# Write a spike's method into its ledger row, step by step

**Learned:** 2026-10-23 · **From:** damage-check build

The O1 spike measured 67,688 ha of 2023's squares as burned, and its code was deleted. At
`/build`, `damage-check-2023` measured 68,826 ha over the same 456,345 ha: 61.8% on peat, not
61.1%. An independent raster count agreed with the command, but nobody could say what the spike
did differently. Every scenario's figures had to change.

When a spike's number goes into a scenario, write its method into the row: the inputs, the test
for "burned", how areas were measured. Treat the number as provisional until the build's command
gives it, and say so in the scenario.
