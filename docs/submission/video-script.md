# Fire Watch: 3-minute demo script

Recorded on https://orange-rock-0f5eae400.1.azurestaticapps.net. Read the narration, not the screen.

## Before recording

- Three tabs. Tab 1: `/`. Tab 2: `/?tool`. Tab 3: `/?pack=ID-WOOD-CONCESSION-0332&fire=0x13b417c6f54dd95c7c418b5df3f34963cf00f46555d5cae74693cdffbb1d1cf7&as_of=1792227600`.
- Open tab 3 a minute early to wake the API. It can take a minute.
- Desktop browser, mouse, window over 720 px wide, reduced motion off, or the globe goes flat or still.
- Reload tab 1 before each take: its seal check runs only once.
- Scroll the case slowly; the map follows the step mid-screen.
- In the print dialog, pick Save as PDF.

## Scenes

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:18 | Tab 1, the headline "When a company says a fire spread in from outside its land…" | Watchdog groups report plantation fires in Indonesia to the companies that buy the wood. The plantation company can answer that the fire came in from outside its land. Then the buyer has two stories and no record. Fire Watch gives them the record. |
| 0:18–0:33 | Click "Go to the example fire". The globe flies to it. | Each dot is fire seen on 14 October by NOAA-20, a US weather satellite run by NOAA. Its detections reach Fire Watch through NASA FIRMS. Here is one fire, beside a pulpwood concession in Central Kalimantan. |
| 0:33–0:50 | Click "Walk through a real case". Scroll past "Two accounts of one fire". | This is the real case. From 12 to 17 October the satellite saw this fire on 8 passes, 283 detections in all. The dashed line is the concession's edge. The company answers for fires inside it. |
| 0:50–1:13 | Scroll to "Where it was first seen": one diamond, outside the dashed line. | Fire Watch's records first show it on 12 October, as one detection, 3,100 metres outside the concession. The page says what that can't prove. The satellite didn't see the fire again for 25 hours, so that detection may be a separate fire. And it misses fire under cloud and smoke. |
| 1:13–1:34 | Scroll "Pass 2 of 8" to "Pass 8 of 8". | Then pass by pass. On the second pass, the next day, another fire appears on both sides of the edge. On pass 5 the two come within a kilometre, so Fire Watch counts them as one. By the end, 164 of the 283 detections were inside. |
| 1:34–1:52 | Scroll to "Every record is sealed". Wait for "283 of 283 detections match the chain". | Each detection's fingerprint is sealed on Base Sepolia, a public test blockchain. Right now my browser reads every seal and compares it with the record. 283 of 283 match. A record changed after its seal would not. |
| 1:52–2:16 | Tab 2. Dates 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-17. Click ID-WOOD-CONCESSION-0332, third. Click the top fire, 12 Oct 05:16. Press play on the map. Print it to PDF for the grievance. |
